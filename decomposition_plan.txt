n brief, the monthly decomposition works as follows:
	•	The General Shift Plan defines how much volume should move in total over the year.
	•	The monthly maturity ladder defines when execution is possible.
	•	Total shifts are first distributed across months based on maturity availability.
	•	Within each month, flows are routed following the proportions in the General Shift Plan (with external flows handled consistently).

⸻

Simple example

Assume the General Shift Plan specifies:
	•	1Y → 6M: 1,200
	•	1Y → 3M:   800

Total 1Y outflow = 2,000.

If the 1Y maturity profile is:
	•	Jan 25%, Feb 35%, Mar 40%

Then monthly execution is:
	•	Jan: 500, Feb: 700, Mar: 800

Within each month, the routing follows the annual ratios:
	•	60% to 6M, 40% to 3M
(e.g. in Jan: 300 to 6M, 200 to 3M).

This ensures monthly execution is feasible and fully reconciles back to the General Shift Plan.
